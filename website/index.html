Frequently asked

Is YuviXterm a MobaXterm alternative for Mac?

Yes. MobaXterm is Windows-only. YuviXterm brings the parts Mac users miss most — a saved-host sidebar, tabbed SSH terminals, and a live remote resource monitor — to a native macOS app.

Is it really free?

Yes, YuviXterm is free to download and use. No account, no subscription.

Does it run on Apple Silicon?

Yes — Apple Silicon (M-series) and Intel Macs, macOS 11 Big Sur or later.

Where are my passwords stored?

Encrypted with the macOS Keychain (via Electron's safeStorage) and never written to disk in plaintext. SSH keys, key passphrases and the ssh-agent are supported too.

macOS says it can't verify the developer — how do I open it?

On first launch, right-click the app in Finder and choose Open, then Open again. macOS remembers the choice from then on.

Does it do SFTP, X11 or port forwarding?

Not yet — YuviXterm is focused on SSH terminals plus the live monitor. File transfer and tunnels are on the roadmap.
